✏️ Doodle Classifier

Build your own tiny AI! You draw the training data. Teach it two kinds of doodles, then test it on new drawings, and discover why good examples matter.

1 · Draw

Draw in the box, then add the doodle to a class, or ask for a guess.

2 · Train

Give your two classes names (e.g. sun / house), then add at least 3 doodles each. Tap a thumbnail to remove it.

💡 The classifier has no idea what a "sun" is. It only compares your new doodle with the examples you gave it, pixel by pixel. You are the dataset.

🧪 Experiments to try

  1. Few vs many: train with 1 doodle per class, test, then add more. Does it get better?
  2. Messy data: sneak a house doodle into the sun class. What happens to the guesses? (That's a labelling error.)
  3. Narrow data: draw all your suns in the top-left corner, then test with a sun in the bottom-right. Why does it fail? (That's skewed data.)
  4. Confusable classes: rename the classes to "circle" and "ball" and try to train them apart. Why is it so hard?
