🗣️ Prompt Duel

A two-player, one-screen game. A language model picks its next word by counting how often words followed each other in its training stories. Can you predict its pick before the counts are revealed?
🔴 Player 1
0
🔵 Player 2

The bars are word counts from the model's "training stories". They stay hidden until both players have picked. Just like a model, you must predict from what feels most likely!

📏 How to play

Each turn, both players pick a different candidate word. Then the counts are revealed: you score that word's count, and the sentence continues with the model's top pick. After the sentence ends, swap who goes first. Highest total wins, and the winner is usually whoever thinks most like the data!
