Codes & Numbers

Meaning as numbers
I know words become numbers, and words that mean similar things get similar numbers (proto-embeddings).
I can group things by their features, and check whether a set of examples is fair.
I care. I'm a data detective who checks the data behind AI.

🌱

Learning Meadow

Rules vs learning from data
I know some tasks follow fixed rules; others are learned from data, even from rewards, by trial and error.
I can train a sorter by refining a rule, testing it and trying again.
I care. I improve by trying, checking and adjusting.
3
🎨

Making Workshop

Predicting & prompting
I know a chatbot writes by predicting a likely next word, and an image generator builds a picture from a description.
I can write a clear prompt (role + instruction + example) and improve it after seeing the result.
I care. I iterate: try, check, and make it better.

⚓

Helper Harbor

Perceive → Decide → Act
I know a helper works as a loop: it perceives, decides, then acts.
I can sketch a system (input → model → output → user) and name one likely failure and a fix.
I care. I design for real people and plan for problems.

🌳

Guardian's Grove

Truth, honesty & impact
I know AI can hallucinate (sound sure but be wrong), and that running it uses real energy and water.
I can cross-check an AI claim against a trusted source, and honestly acknowledge AI help.
I care. I check, I'm honest, and I think about AI's impact on people and the planet.
