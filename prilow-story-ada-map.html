Ada's AI Adventure

Follow Ada and her puppy Ida across five lands. In each one she collects what she knows, what she can do, and what she cares about: the building blocks of understanding AI.

I know… (knowledge) I can… (skill) I care… (disposition)
1
🔢

Codes & Numbers

Turning things into numbers
I know computers use numbers, not letters, and AI is a program people build, not alive.
I can crack a number code and sort things by a shared feature.
I care about finding the pattern, not calling it magic.
2
🌱

Learning Meadow

Teaching a machine from examples
I know AI learns from many examples: lots of cat pictures teach it “cat”.
I can continue a pattern and follow steps like a robot to reach the goal.
I care about keeping trying and checking.
3
🎨

Making Workshop

Creating vs finding things
I know the difference between making something new (generative AI) and finding what already exists.
I can play “what comes next?” and feel how prediction works.
I care about how new things are made; I stay curious.
4
⚓

Helper Harbor

Sense → Think → Act
I know a helper machine works as a loop: it senses, thinks, then acts.
I can ask a clear, kind question and design my own helper robot.
I care about designing with kindness and safety.
5
🌳

Guardian's Grove

Safety, honesty & kindness
I know to keep private things private, and that AI can sometimes be wrong.
I can check with a trusted adult and choose the safer, kinder action.
I care about being honest and checking before I trust.
🏅 Finish all five lands to become a Junior AI Explorer!
